07 / QUESTIONS

Questions clients ask before signing.

How does the payment model work?

Initial fixed fee + Success Fee that varies based on your industry (more complex sectors or higher-ticket deals have a higher Success Fee, because they require more research, personalization and conversion time). The Success Fee is paid only on qualified meetings the prospect actually attended. No annual contract, no exit penalty. The exact numbers for your industry are discussed on the diagnostic call.

What stays mine after 120 days?

Everything. Dedicated email domains, warmed mailboxes, multichannel sequences, multilingual copy, segmented lists, CRM integrations, dashboard. It's an acquisition infrastructure that's yours. You can continue alone, with us at reduced retainer, or replace the team.

How does it cost vs hiring internally?

An internal Senior SDR costs €40-50K/year + benefits + 6 months training + €1,500/month software. Storm X is operational in 14 days instead of 6 months and includes tools, infrastructure and senior team. Pipeline target is 5–10× the program cost.

Do you only work in Italy?

No. Italy, UK, France, Spain, Germany, UAE. Native copywriting in the prospect's language, market-segmented databases. Other markets need a 1-month discovery. The Isolcore case: 11 campaigns in 6 countries simultaneously.

Who do you work with?

B2B SMEs with validated product/service and average ticket > €5,000-10,000. Mainly tech scale-ups, B2B software, consulting agencies, medical devices, food export. We don't accept everyone: only companies that can handle the volumes we generate.
