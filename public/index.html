Before we start

Let’s make
things clear.

Which stages of the sales process can you manage?+

From account research and first contact to qualification, calls, proposals, follow-up and support through to a decision. We agree which stages Storm X manages and which stay with your team.

Can you work alongside our sales team?+

Yes. We can support the areas your team wants to strengthen, share opportunity information and agree who owns each step.

How do we start, and what does it cost?+

We begin with a free call to understand your offer and goals. We then prepare a proposal covering activities, responsibilities, timing and commercial terms, based on the work required.

How soon can we expect results?+

It depends on the market, offer and sales cycle. We agree the launch stages and track replies, conversations and opportunities. The case studies show the results and duration of individual projects; they are not forecasts for every business.

Does the first conversation commit us to working together?+

No. The first conversation is free and helps us assess whether we can help. If there is a good fit, we prepare a proposal with a scope and terms for you to review before starting.
