01 / Choose the scope

The right support for your team.

01

Acquisition and appointments

You already have a sales team. We research target companies, open conversations, qualify interest and arrange a clear handover to your salespeople.

02

Full sales cycle

You need broader support. We also help with sales calls, follow-ups, proposals and negotiations through to a decision and the handover of a new customer to your team.

02 / A measurable starting point

120-day pilot.

We start with an agreed scope: offer, markets, people to reach, activities and measures. The pilot helps us understand which conversations create opportunities and how to develop them.

  1. PREPARATION

    Let's focus on the market.

    Offer, ideal customer, messages, tools and responsibilities. We agree the qualification criteria before launch.

  2. LAUNCH

    Let's open the first conversations.

    Research, coordinated outreach and reply handling. We choose channels according to the person and context.

  3. DEVELOPMENT

    Review, adapt and progress.

    Meetings, opportunities and negotiations within the agreed scope. We use feedback to improve the work.

This sequence is indicative. Start dates, activities and delivery arrangements are defined in the proposal. Sales cycles vary by sector, product and market.

03 / Clear responsibility

One path. Two teams working together.

Storm X

  • Research, contact and conversation management.
  • Qualification, coordination and follow-up.
  • Updates on activity and next steps.
  • Proposals and negotiations, where included.

Your team

  • Knowledge of your product and ideal customers.
  • Materials, prices and commercial conditions.
  • Technical expertise needed to answer buyer questions.
  • Approval of offers, contracts and delivery.

04 / How to evaluate the project

We measure progress at each stage.

01

Quality of contacts

Companies and roles relevant to the agreed market.

02

Opportunity progression

Conversations, qualified meetings and agreed next steps.

03

Sales outcomes

Opportunities, pipeline and closed sales, reported separately.

We define “qualified” in advance and agree who owns each stage. Pipeline is not an order; reporting keeps the distinction clear.

05 / The proposal

An investment defined around the project.

The cost depends on the markets, complexity of the offer and level of support. The written proposal specifies the retainer, any variable component, review criteria and terms. The initial call is free and helps us understand your context.
