01 / The starting point

The starting point: a new European presence.

RMS International began with an account generating no revenue. The project developed its Amazon presence in Italy, Germany and France, coordinating product launches and advertising across the three markets.

The project reports over €15,000 in revenue in Italy, over €18,000 in Germany and over €18,500 in France in less than three months. Reported volumes are 900, 1,200 and 1,100 units respectively.

Historical project figures for the three marketplaces. They are not monthly recurring revenue. Advertising spend and contribution margins are not published, so these figures alone do not establish profitability.
