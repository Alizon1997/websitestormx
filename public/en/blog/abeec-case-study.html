============ 01 BACKGROUND ============
01 / Context

An English brand of accessible toys, unknown outside its market.

Abeec is an e-commerce company of toys and creative activities at affordable prices, built on a simple idea: the fun of children should not cost a fortune. They sell toys, articles for drawing, lavoretti and cartoleria of quality at prices at the reach of all — own products and lines of known brands.

The range is wide: stationery, table games, soap bubbles, children's cosmetics, colors, painting and creative activities. But European marketplaces already had their industry leaders. To grow outside the United Kingdom, Abeec did not need a translation — it needed a system of entry into the market.

============ 02 CHALLENGE ============
02 / The Challenge

Generate real sales in a market that He never heard you mention.

The main challenge was to generate sales volume and turnover in a foreign market saturated with already established competitors. Abeec had to conquer new customers starting from scratch — while improving operational efficiency and managing the complexity of the supply chain typical of a European expansion.

A brand, online and offline, completely unknown in three new countries.

No presence, no reviews, no positioning history. The plan had to build credibility, visibility and conversion in parallel — not one after the other.

============ 03 APPROACH ============
03 / The approach

A multi-dimensional plan, built on market data.

We started from a complete analysis of the target market and its potential — size, customer needs, competitive density — and then run on three coordinated workflows.

01 / Catalogue

Catalog Management

An initial catalog of 40 ASIN, optimized in every detail for each marketplace.

  • Search for trend keywords by market (IT · FR · DE)
  • SEO-optimized titles and bullet points — a catalog "ready for the customer"
  • Strategic expansion on consolidated lines in EU (e.g. Barbie after the film)
  • Extended selection of +100% in 3 months
02 / Contents

Marketing and design

Premium creativity that speaks the language of each market — literally.

  • Premium A+ content, infographics and copywriting for each marketplace
  • Localized and recharged images in the language of each market
  • Data-driven Main Image Test for Effective Creativity
  • Owner SEO for positioning, indexing and organic traffic
03 / Advertising

Advertising

Campaigns curated in detail, managed with agility to vary the seasons.

  • Targeting on primary keywords, direct and indirect competitors
  • Targeting for category and video content
  • Defensive strategies against loss of customers to other cards
  • PPC Expenditure re-allocated frequently to maximise return
