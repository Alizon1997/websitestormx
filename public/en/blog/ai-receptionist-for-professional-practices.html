AI phone call office for professional studies: how much it costs Really.

Doctors, dentists, lawyers, accountants: those who answer the phone when the study is full — and what changes, in practice and by law, if to answer is an AI.

Published on 2026-08-17

TL;DR — A AI phone secretariat for professional studies (medical, dentist, legal, accountants) responds 24/7, book and reschedule appointments on the real agenda, reduces missed appointments with automatic reminders and turns human only requests that you do not have to manage alone. It costs 100-400 EUR/month of platform plus 0.05-0,15 EUR per minute, with an initial setup of 1,500-5.000 EUR — almost always less than a part-time receptionist if the volume of calls justifies it. From 10 October 2025 Law 132/2025 requires that patients and clients be informed clearly when it is an AI to respond.

If you run a medical, dental, legal or consultancy study, you will sooner or later ask the same question: telephone call AI Do you really hold up for a professional study, or is it just another demo that sounds good until someone asks for something out of the script? The question makes sense, because a study is not a call center: who often calls is in anxiety, has hurry, or expects the right tone — not only a quick answer.

This guide goes into detail what actually manages an AI secretariat in a professional study, how much it costs compared to alternatives, what changes with the obligation of information introduced by 2025 rules on artificial intelligence, and when — honestly — it does not have to be installed yet.

What is an AI voicemail for a professional study (and where it ends)

A AI voice assistant applied to a professional study is a system that answers the phone with natural voice, understands the reason of the call and acts accordingly: it takes an appointment, responds to a recurring question, collects a structured message or turns the call to a person. Technically it is the same type of system described in our general article on AI control unit for companies, but applied to a context with precise characteristics: few decision-makers, many repeated requests, and a relationship of trust — doctor-patient, lawyer-client — that cannot be treated as any ticket.

It is not, and it must not prove to be, a substitute for professional judgment. It does not diagnose, does not give legal opinions, does not decide clinical priorities. Fa da front desk: the repetitive and predictable work part that today occupies hours of secretariat and that, if managed badly or late, costs lost appointments and patients or clients who call another study.

The tasks that really manages (and those that must always turn to a human)

In the implementations we see working, the list of what an AI secretariat does well in a professional study is short and concrete:

  • Book and reschedule appointments seeing free slots in real time on the study agenda.
  • Send automatic reminders the day before, reducing missed appointments — the most underrated cost item in many studies.
  • Responding to recurring questions: times, address, documents to be carried, payment methods, if the study accepts a certain type of insurance or convention.
  • Collect a structured message when the request does not fall into the script, by name, contact and reason of the call ready for those who call after.
  • Filter commercial calls (suppliers, sellers, unsolicited proposals) without losing time to those who really work with patients or customers.

And two things that must always turn to a person, without exception: The real urges (a serious symptom, an imminent legal deadline, any situation where a delay has a serious cost) and complaints or delicate situations, where tone and human judgment count more than speed. A well configured AI script recognizes these cases from keywords and passes the call immediately, instead of trying to manage them yourself.

How much does an AI voicemail cost for a study (honest comparison)

For a study, the realistic comparison is between three options: one person in the studio (also part-time), a traditional external voicemail service, or an AI secretariat.

The AI solution numbers are market benchmark 2025-2026 based on the public prices of major providers (Vapi, Retell AI, ElevenLabs) — the same comparison, with the complete detail for a generic SME, is in our article on the AI control unit for companies. For a study with 1-2 professionals, a typical volume can be around 150-250 minutes of calls per month (just tens of calls per day, short): at that level the variable cost remains contained, indicating 10-35 EUR/month of only API, and the operating total (platform included) is around 110-435 EUR/month — under the cost of a part-time receptionist, but with the initial setup to be dammed in the first months of use. Under that volume threshold, the account changes: if the study receives few calls per day, the economic advantage is thinned and sometimes there is not.

By comparison, the cost of a fully loaded human operator (rependent, contributions, management) is around 18 EUR/hour — it is one of the reasons why, at the same time covered hours, the AI model costs less as soon as the volume rises.

The obligation to inform: what changes with the Law 132/2025

Since 10 October 2025 the Law No 132/2025, the first organic Italian legislation on artificial intelligence, coordinated with EU Regulation 2024/1689 (AI Act). Article 13 introduces a point that directly affects those who install an AI secretariat in a professional study: those who use artificial intelligence systems in their activity must to inform clearly, simple and exhaustive customers (or patients) on the methods and purposes with which AI is used, and AI can only have a support role — never replace the evaluation and direct responsibility of the professional. The obligation explicitly concerns lawyers, notaries and accountants, and has also been confirmed for dentists and other health professions.

In practice, for a study that adopts an AI phone secretariat this translates into two concrete things: to update the privacy / assignment information by mentioning the use of AI in the management of calls, and — good practice regardless of the obligation — to declare to the assistant himself, at the beginning of the call, that it is an automatic system ("I am the voice assistant of the study"), instead of letting the caller discover it alone. It is not legal advice: informational obligations change according to the professional order and must be verified with your advisor or your order before going into production.

The pattern we see working better in the studies is not "AI instead of the person", but AI to cover the recurring volume and extra hours, with a person who remains the reference for all that requires judgment. In many cases the AI secretariat removes precisely the most boring and repetitive calls from the load of those who work in the studio, leaving more time for the part that really requires a person.

When it is (and when not) for a professional study

Makes sense when:

  • The study receives a regular volume of repetitive requests (booking, reprogramming, questions on times and documents).
  • There are more professionals or multiple locations, and lost calls are translated into lost appointments.
  • Missed appointments (no-show) are a real economic problem and automatic reminders can reduce them.
  • The study wants to cover hourly bands today discovered (breakfast, evening, weekend) without hiring.

It doesn't matter when:

  • The volume of calls is very low: the setup is not damaging in reasonable times.
  • Almost every call requires a custom assessment that no script can predict.
  • The study does not (or does not want to build) a digital agenda linkable — without integration, the value falls much.

How to integrate with the study agenda and management

The real value is not in "responding to the phone with natural voice" — that is the basic level. It is in the link with the agenda (Google Calendar, Outlook, Cal.com or the specific management of the study) to see free slots, really book, and update data without anyone having to copy them by hand. For industry managers who do not have a ready integration, it is necessary to build a tailor-made link: it is the same work we do with the automatons Make.com to connect CRMs, calendars and vertical systems, and that in some cases requires dedicated development via custom software when the management does not expose standard APIs.

From where to start

  1. Measure the real volume of calls for a week: how many come, how many remain unanswered, how many repetitive requests that could follow a fixed script.
  2. Write the script before you choose the platform. Questions to ask, requests to always turn to a human, limit cases: without this, any technology produces mediocre results.
  3. Update the information together with your advisor, before going to production, not after.

FAQ: AI phone secretariat for professional studies

How much does an AI voicemail cost for a medical or professional study?

Based on the public prices of the main providers (Vapi, Retell AI, ElevenLabs), the variable cost is approximately 0.05-0,15 EUR per minute, plus a 100-400 EUR/month platform and an initial setup of 1,500-5.000 EUR. For a study with 1-2 professionals and limited volumes, the monthly operating cost remains almost always under that of a part-time receptionist (indicatively 800-1,500 EUR/month), but the setup is only shocked if the volume of calls justifies it.

Can an AI voicemail make real appointments on the study calendar?

Yes, if it is connected to the agenda or the management of the study: it sees free slots, proposes a date, confirmation and updates the calendar in real time, as well as sending automatic reminders to reduce missed appointments. Without this integration there is only one switchboard that collects messages, with much less practical value.

Should I inform patients and clients if answering is artificial intelligence?

From October 10, 2025, Law 132/2025 requires professionals using artificial intelligence systems in their business to clearly, simple and exhaustively inform customers (or patients) about the methods and purposes of use, and clarifies that AI can only have a supporting role, never replace the evaluation of the professional. It is not legal advice: verify the exact text and specific obligations of your professional category with your advisor or your order.

An AI secretariat completely replaces the human secretary of the study?

In most studies, no: it manages repetitive requests (booking, reprogramming, information on times and documents) but it must always be configured to turn to a human real clinical or legal urgencies, delicate complaints and requests coming out of the script. The model that works best is complementary: the AI covers the recurring volume and extra times, a person remains the reference point for everything else.

Does it also work out of time, on weekends and during the study holidays?

Yes, it is one of the main reasons why a study adopts it: it responds 24/7, so also at lunch, in the evening, on weekends and during closures for holidays, when today the call falls empty or ends in the classic voicemail without action.

What types of professional studies can use an AI phone secretariat?

Any study with a recurring volume of repetitive requests on the phone: medical and polyambulatory studies, dental studies, legal studies, accountants, notaries, technical studies and consultancy. It is more convenient where the demands are standardizable (booking, reprogramming, asking for times and documents); it is less convenient in micro-studies with very few calls per day or where each request always requires a customized evaluation.
