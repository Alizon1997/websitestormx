How to automate business withAID in 2026

Operating Framework to identify which processes automate first, calculate the expected ROI, avoid the errors we see most often.

Published on 2026-03-31

Corporate automation based on artificial intelligence is no longer a luxury reserved for large companies. In 2026, Italian SMEs can also leverage AI to reduce operating costs, eliminate repetitive activities and scale their business without increasing staff. In this guide we explain how to do, step by step.

What You Can Automate With AI in Your Company

The first mistake many entrepreneurs make is to think that AI automation only concerns chatbots. Actually, almost every repetitive process can be automated. Here are the areas with the greatest impact for SMEs:

Lead and Sales Management

AI can automatically qualify incoming leads, assign a priority score, send custom follow-ups and update CRM in real time. Your business team focuses only on the most promising prospects, without wasting time with cold contacts. Learn more about our AI automation for business.

Customer Service

AI voice agents and smart chatbots can handle 80% of customer requests without human intervention: answers to frequently asked questions, appointment booking, order status updates. The escalation to an operator only happens when really necessary.

Marketing and Communication

From the automatic segmentation of the public to the customization of emails, from the analysis of country data to the generation of weekly reports. AI makes marketing more effective and less expensive in terms of time.

Operations and Administration

Data entry automatic, synchronization data between different software, document generation, billing and accounting reconciliation. Everything that today requires manual working hours can be completed in seconds.

The Right Tools for Automating

You don't need any millionaire investment or development team. In 2026 there are powerful and accessible tools that allow to create sophisticated automations:

Make.com: No-Code Visive Automation

Make.com is the platform we use for most automations. It allows you to connect hundreds of applications (CRM, email, social media, database) and create complex workflows with a drag-and-drop visual interface. Compared to Zapier, it offers greater flexibility and lower costs for multi-step automation.

Claude Code: Personalized AI Development

For more advanced automations requiring custom artificial intelligence, we use Claude Code by Anthropic. This tool allows you to develop customized AI solutions: document analysis, content generation, data classification, advanced conversational agents.

APIs and Integrations

Every effective automation is based on solid integrations with your existing tools: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack and dozens of other tools. We do not ask you to change your tools: we integrate with what you already use.

The ROI of AI Automation for SMEs

The numbers speak clear. This is what Italian SMEs typically see that they implement AI automations:

  • Reduction of 40-60% of operating costs on automated activities
  • Save 15-30 hours per week for team of 5-10 people
  • Positive ROI within 60 days from implementation
  • Increase of 25-35% of conversion rate thanks to timely and personalized follow-up
  • Zero Errors data entry and data synchronization activities

The cost of NOT automating is much higher than the cost of implementation. Every month that passes without automation means lost hours, missed opportunities and unnecessary costs.

How to Start: The 4 Steps to Automate Your Business

1. Process mapping

The first step is to identify all repetitive processes in your company. What activities are carried out every day, every week? Where do you waste more hours? Where do you make more mistakes? This analysis is essential to prioritize automation.

2. Quick Win Identification

You don't need to automate everything right away. Starting from the "quick win": simple automations with high impact. For example, automating email follow-ups on leads can only take a week of implementation but generate immediate results.

3. Implementation and Testing

Each automation is built, tested and optimized before being put into production. We use an iterative approach: we start with a basic workflow and gradually improve it according to the results.

4. Monitoring and Optimization

Automations are not "set and forget". We constantly monitor performance, identify bottlenecks and optimize workflows to maximize results over time.
