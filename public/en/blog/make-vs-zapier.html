Make.com vs Zapier: the comparison operating operating operations

We migrated 47 scenarios from Zapier to Make.com in the last year. What we learned: real prices, performance, edge cases.

Published on 2026-04-05

If you're looking for an automation platform for your company, you've definitely come across two names: Make.com (formerly Integromat) and Zapier. Both allow you to automate business processes by connecting your applications, but have substantial differences. In this comparison updated to 2026 we help you understand which to choose.

What is Make.com and What is Zapier

Make.com is a visual automation platform that allows you to create complex workflows with a drag-and-drop editor. Each automation is represented as a visual flow diagram, making it easy to understand and change even the most articulated processes.

Zapier is the most known automation platform in the world. It works with a linear system "trigger-action": when X happens, do Y. It is extremely simple to use for basic automations and has the widest integration catalog on the market.

Advantages of Make.com

Superior Visive Interface

Make.com's visual editor is his main strength. You can see the entire automation flow as a diagram, with ramifications, cycles, filters and error management. For complex workflows with more than 3-4 steps, this display is essential to maintain control. Find out how we use Make.com in our automatons Make.com.

Flexibility in Complex Workflows

Make.com natively supports conditional ramifications (router), cycles (iterator and aggregator), advanced error management (error handler on each module) and custom variables. With Zapier, to get the same result they often serve complex workarounds or more separate Zap.

Very cheap price

Make.com counts "operations" (every steps performed), while Zapier counts "task". At equal workflow, Make.com averages 60-80% less than Zapier. For a company that performs thousands of automations per month, saving is significant. Make.com's free plan includes 1,000 operations per month, enough to test and start the first automations.

Data Control and HTTP/API

Make.com has powerful native HTTP modules that allow you to connect to any API, even without a dedicated integration. You can manipulate data with advanced features, parsare JSON, manage webhook and create custom integrations without writing code.

Advantages of Zapier

Simplicity of Use

Zapier is easier to configure for linear automation. The step-by-step interface is intuitive even for those who do not have technical skills. If you need simple automation (when I receive an email, save the attachment on Google Drive), Zapier is the fastest choice.

Catalogue of Larger Integrations

Zapier supports over 6,000 applications, compared to about 1,500 of Make.com. If you use niche tools or uncommon platforms, it is more likely to find native integration on Zapier. However, Make.com covers all major business applications and allows you to connect to any service via HTTP/webhook.

Ecosystem and Community

Zapier has a larger community, more ready-to-use templates and more training resources. For those who start from scratch and want simple automation, the Zapier ecosystem offers a more guided learning path.

When to Choose Make.com

  • Complex workflow with ramifications, multiple conditions and error management
  • High voltages of executions (the cost per operation is much lower)
  • Personalized integrations via API and webhook
  • Advanced data manipulation: parsing, transformation, aggregation
  • Multi-step automation with more than 5 steps
  • Budget limited but high automation needs

When to choose Zapier

  • Simple automation and linear (trigger + 1-2 actions)
  • Niche Tool not supported by Make.com
  • Non-technical team that must independently manage automations
  • Quick Prototyping to test an idea in a few minutes

Why Storm X Digital Use Make.com

In our experience with hundreds of automations for Italian and European SMEs, Make.com is the best choice in the vast majority of cases. The main reasons:

  • Real automations are almost always complex. Even a simple lead management process requires conditions, ramifications and error management that Make.com manages natively.
  • The cost scales better. As a company automates more processes, saving compared to Zapier becomes huge.
  • The visual interface facilitates maintenance. When a workflow stops working, Make.com identifies the problem in seconds. With Zapier, the complex Zap debug is much more frustrating.
  • API flexibility is fundamental. Many integrations we build for customers require custom HTTP connections that Make.com handles brilliantly.

Final verdict

If your company needs automations that grow over time, they manage complex processes and must remain sustainable in terms of cost, Make.com is the right choice. If you need to connect two apps with simple automation and do not expect to scale, Zapier can be enough.

Our advice: start with Make.com. The learning curve is slightly steeper, but the medium-long term advantage is huge, both in terms of flexibility and cost.
