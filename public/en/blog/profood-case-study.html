============ 01 CLIENT ============
01 / The customer

Export of fresh fruit and vegetables, with table grapes in the center.

Profood is an Italian company specializing inexport of fresh fruit and vegetables. The main focus is table grapes, flanked by an assortment of seasonal products.

It focuses on a network of producers, continuity of supply all year round and quality of Italian origin. He wanted to open up new foreign markets without building an internal commercial team.

============ 03 CHALLENGE ============
03 / The Challenge

They count relationships with the right buyers, not email volumes.

In the orthoprutta B2B talk with the wrong decision maker is time lost. Profood had to get to buyers, CEOs and category managers of importers and distributors already active on the fresh.

In Europe first, then in the Middle East. New markets, zero internal team.

The goal was not to send more messages, but to build relationships that, in the fresh, close the order — and make them repeat over time.

============ 04 APPROACH ============
04 / What we did

A system in four moves, from cold contact at the fair.

Database built from zero, geographic expansion to step, multichannel outbound with AI and exhibition support — each phase feeds the next.

01 / Database

Database of decision maker

A list of real contacts, built from scratch and qualified one to one.

  • Cross sources: Zipmec, Best Food Importers and LinkedIn
  • At least one decision maker for company: buyers, CEO, category managers, purchases
  • Qualified list, with priority to those who already work fresh or premium lines
02 / Geography

Geographical strategy in step

First the pilot countries, then the climbed — not the whole world in a single blow.

  • Netherlands and Poland as pilot markets, very active on import
  • Test on offer angles, tone and follow up types
  • Scaled to the rest of Europe and then to the Middle East, where premium demand is high
03 / Outbound + AI

Multichannel Outbound and AI

At least 12 touchpoints for decision makers, adapted with AI to context and season.

  • Email as the main channel, LinkedIn and WhatsApp where there was the coupling
  • Customized messages for company type, country, assortment and market lever
  • Real levers of the field: continuity of quality, logistics, delays to the ports
  • Reminder aligned with seasonality, before the raisin campaign
04 / Fairs

Support for fairs

At Brand and Fruit Attraction not empty-handed, but with the already full agenda.

  • Outreach pre-fare to heat contacts in targets
  • Precise slots proposed to importers and distributors
  • Cold contacts turned into face-to-face meetings
