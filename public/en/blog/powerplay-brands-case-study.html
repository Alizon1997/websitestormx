============ 01 CLIENT ============
01 / The customer

Reebok Socks exclusive licensor, ready to grow in EMEA.

Brands Cosmos is the exclusive licensee of Reebok Socks for the EMEA area. It manages distribution, design, marketing and production for brands that lead to international markets.

He wanted to grow in North Africa, Middle East and CIS. But without building a sales force in every single country — and without diluting control over the brand.

Brand licensed Reebok Socks Brands Cosmos · licensee EMEA
============ 02 CHALLENGE ============
02 / The Challenge

Open new markets without inflate fixed costs.

Open new markets with a distributed sales team costs time and money: six dedicated export managers are a heavy fixed cost, year after year.

They needed conversations with the right retailers, in their language.

Brands Cosmos was looking for a leaner model, able to scale quickly — without sacrificing the quality of relationships or the control of the brand.

============ 03 APPROACH ============
03 / What we did

technological speed and human presence in the negotiations.

Two engines working together: a multichannel outbound system to open conversations, and a broken export manager to bring them to the signature.

01 / Outbound Smart System

Outbound engine on three channels

Email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, with each message adapted to the language and context of each market.

  • Dedicated and heated domains to arrive in inbox, not in spam
  • Multichannel sequences coordinated to the right retailers decision makers
  • Custom audio notes where they serve — they convert more than text
  • Messages in the local language of North Africa, Middle East and CIS
02 / Export manager fractional

From the first contact to the signature

We did not stop at the first contact: we participated in the meetings and managed the negotiations on behalf of the customer.

  • A unique commercial reference, without the cost of six assumptions
  • Negotiations conducted until the closure of the order
  • Brand control maintained by Brands Cosmos in every communication
  • Unchanged fixed costs, quickly scaled commercial capacity
