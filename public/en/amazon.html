Before we start

A few answers.

Can we start from scratch?+

Yes. We can support account opening and catalogue preparation. Your team remains responsible for coordinating documents, brand requirements, compliance and stock.

What if the account is already active?+

We start with the available data and the main priorities: catalogue, content, campaigns and operations. We agree the scope before making changes.

Who prepares and ships the stock?+

Storm X prepares FBA shipping plans and follows Amazon cases. Physical preparation and shipping remain with your team or the agreed logistics partner.

Are advertising, Amazon fees and management the same cost?+

They are separate items. We distinguish the advertising budget, Amazon costs and management fees when assessing the project.
