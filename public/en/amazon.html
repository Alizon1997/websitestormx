06 / Frequently asked questions

Let's make it clear.

Can you work on an existing account?

Yes. We review your Seller or Vendor account, identify priorities and agree on the activities we will manage.

Do you also manage content and advertising?

Yes. Product listings, brand content, SEO and Amazon Advertising are part of our work, within the agreed scope.

Can you guarantee sales or a search ranking?

We agree on objectives and indicators, but results depend on your product, market, offer and account. Our case studies describe specific results and are not a guarantee.

Are Amazon and B2B sales the same service?

They are different services that we can coordinate. Marketplace work covers the account and channel sales. B2B work covers research, outreach, negotiation and closing.

What does the first free analysis include?

An initial discussion of your product, account, markets, content and advertising to identify priorities and where we can help. The full project scope is defined in our proposal.
