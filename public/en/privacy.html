How we process website data and enquiries about your project.

Who is responsible for your data?

Storm X Digital S.R.L. · Via Strada del Carro 24, 76011 Bisceglie (BA), Italy · VAT and tax identification number 08789080721.

Contact: marcellodipierro@stormxdigital.com. Certified email (PEC): stormxdigital@pec.it.

Your enquiry: data and purposes

The form collects your name, email address, company and the message you choose to send. We also store the date, reference, acknowledgement of the privacy notice and handling status. This information is used to respond to your enquiry, assess the project and arrange a call. The legal basis is taking pre-contractual steps at the request of the data subject (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Required fields are necessary to handle the enquiry; submitting the form does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

To limit abusive submissions, we use a daily identifier derived from the network address and store it as a hash associated with the enquiry. We do not use it for profiling. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the security of the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How your enquiry is handled

The public website is hosted on Vercel. Contact-form services, the database and the private dashboard are managed through Sites on Cloudflare infrastructure. Enquiries are stored in the website database and made available to the data controller in the private area. An n8n workflow forwards the content to Marcello’s mailbox through the connected email service. The data controller, authorised personnel and suppliers involved in technical delivery can access the data for their respective roles.

Suppliers may also operate outside the European Economic Area. The relevant processing and transfers are subject to the conditions and safeguards applicable to the services used. We do not sell enquiry data.

Retention periods

Commercial enquiries are retained for up to 24 months from the last contact, except where there is an ongoing collaboration, a legal obligation or a need to protect legal rights. Data required for contracts and tax documents follows the applicable statutory retention periods. Aggregated website statistics are retained for up to 14 months.

Optional statistics without individual profiles

Only if you choose to accept statistics do we record daily counts of page views, clicks on main actions and video playback. The statistics system does not store a visitor identifier, email addresses, form contents, URL parameters or IP addresses. We do not calculate unique visitors. Your preference is stored in the browser for six months and can be changed using the “Statistics preferences” link in the footer. The legal basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Submitted enquiries are recorded regardless of your statistics choice because they are needed to respond to you. The private area distinguishes these enquiries from optional statistical counts.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your consent to statistics at any time, without affecting processing already carried out. Contact the data controller using the details above. You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority: Garanteprivacy.it.

Booking and anti-bot verification

Booking opens Marcello’s Cal.com calendar, which processes the information needed for the appointment under its own privacy notice. The website does not pass form data to Cal.com: you enter it in the calendar only if you choose to book. For the form and access to the calendar, we use a single-use verification code valid for five minutes, also available in an audio version. The code, token and daily network-address hash are used to prevent abuse; expired records are removed when the next code is issued. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the security of the service.

Optional source counts

With statistics consent, we also count referring-source categories: Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, other or unknown. Only the category is stored, not the referring URL or search query. A missing referrer is recorded as unknown. These counts do not identify people or distinguish paid from organic traffic.
