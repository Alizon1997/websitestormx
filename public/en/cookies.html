Information about browser storage and the tools used on this website.

Necessary resources and browser storage

The website uses resources necessary for navigation and security. Access to the private area uses the hosting service’s authentication. We store your statistics preference in the browser’s localStorage for six months so that we can respect your choice.

Optional statistics

Statistics remain disabled until you choose “Accept statistics”. We record only daily counts of page views, clicks and videos, without personal identifiers or tracking between websites. Choosing “Necessary only” keeps this measurement disabled. You can change your choice from the footer. The form and videos also work without accepting statistics.

Videos, fonts and external links

Videos, images, fonts and anti-bot verification are served directly from the website. Booking opens Cal.com only when you choose to continue to the calendar; its own privacy notice then applies. We do not embed external players or social buttons that load third-party services when the page opens. Links to LinkedIn and other websites open those services only when you select them; their own privacy notices apply.

No advertising tracking

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel and advertising profiling tools are not active in this version. Any new services will require an updated notice and, where necessary, a choice before activation.

Optional source counts

With statistics consent, we also count referring-source categories: Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, other or unknown. Only the category is stored, not the referring URL or search query. A missing referrer is recorded as unknown. These counts do not identify people or distinguish paid from organic traffic.
