Information about browser storage and the tools used on this website.

01 / Operation

Technical memories.

The site uses the resources necessary for navigation and safety. Access to the reserved area uses authentication of the hosting service. In the browser we save preference on statistics in localStorage, with a six-month deadline. The preference is to respect your choice.

02 / Your choice

Optional statistics.

Statistics are deactivated until you choose “Accept statistics”. We only record daily counts of pages, clicks and videos, without personal identifiers or tracking between sites. “Only necessary” lets this measurement be disabled. You can change your choice from the footer. The module and the videos also work without accepting statistics.

03 / External Content

Videos, fonts and links.

Videos, images, characters and anti-bot verification are served directly from the site. The reservation opens Cal.com only when you choose to continue to the calendar; your information applies. We do not incorporate external players or social buttons that upload third-party services to the opening of the page. Links to LinkedIn and other sites open their services only when selecting them; they apply their information.

04 / Active tools

No advertising tracking.

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel or advertising profiling tools are not active in this version. Any new services will require an update of information and, where necessary, a choice before their activation.
