Storm X Digital / B2B

Selling abroad takes follow-through.

Entering a market means finding relevant buyers, understanding their context and progressing each opportunity. We work alongside your sales team from buyer and distributor research through proposals, follow-ups and negotiations, with agreed responsibilities and commercial terms.

One process. Clear responsibilities.

Research

Agree countries, channels, target companies and decision-makers. Build a verifiable account list that fits your product and sales capacity.

Conversations

Coordinate email, LinkedIn and phone with relevant messages. Qualify interest, role, timing and the next step.

Opportunity development

Prepare a handover to your team or support proposals and negotiations. Prices, concessions and terms are agreed with you.

Visibility

Share activities and opportunity status. Separate contacts, meetings, pipeline and signed business: they measure different things.
