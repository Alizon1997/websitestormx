From a list to a conversation.

B2B lead generation identifies companies that may need your offer and opens a conversation with decision-makers. At Storm X, a contact list is only the starting point: we manage outreach and explore replies before handing opportunities to sales.

What we do.

Define the target

We start with your product, value proposition and ideal customers. We agree on sectors, countries, company size and the roles to reach.

Start conversations

Company research, relevant messaging and coordinated email, LinkedIn and phone outreach. The next step reflects the replies we receive.

Qualify interest

We explore needs and context, share useful information and arrange a sales handover against agreed criteria.

What does “qualified” mean?

A positive reply does not automatically mean a buyer is ready to purchase. Before launch we agree what to check: company fit, contact role, expressed need and willingness to take a next step. Budget, timing and buying authority are explored where the context allows.

Reporting separates companies reached, replies, relevant conversations and meetings. Your sales team receives the conversation context and agreed next step, rather than just a name.

Who follows up after the meeting?

If you have a sales team, we arrange a clear handover and collect feedback on opportunity quality. If you need support through to a decision, proposals, follow-ups and negotiation can be included in a sales development engagement.

Questions before we begin.

Do you sell contact databases?

This is an acquisition service covering research, messaging, reply handling and qualification. Activities and information shared are defined in the engagement.

Do you work in international markets?

Yes. We assess countries, languages, contacts and feasibility for your offer. Previous experience is described in the case studies.

Do you guarantee meetings or customers?

We agree on objectives and qualification criteria, but cannot guarantee prospect interest, purchases or decision times.

What do you need to get started?

Your offer, priority markets, ideal customer profile and available sales materials. We review them together and agree on scope before starting.
