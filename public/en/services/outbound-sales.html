An external sales team.

B2B sales development connects finding new customers with progressing open opportunities. Storm X Digital supports businesses entering new markets or seeking a consistent sales process without building a dedicated department from scratch.

From first contact to signed contract.

Research and outreach

We agree on markets, target companies and decision-makers. We coordinate relevant email, LinkedIn and phone outreach. WhatsApp is considered when the contact has agreed to use that channel.

Qualification and proposals

We explore needs and interest, arrange meetings and support sales calls. We work with you on proposals that fit the opportunity.

Negotiation and decision

We manage replies, follow-ups and negotiation. Your team approves prices, terms and contracts; responsibilities are agreed for each stage.

Before we start.

Do you cover the full sales cycle?

We can support research, outreach, qualification, meetings, proposals and negotiation through to a decision. Scope is agreed with your team; pricing and contractual terms remain subject to your approval.

Can we keep our existing sales team?

Yes. We can handle acquisition and hand qualified opportunities to your salespeople, or continue supporting them in later stages.

When should we expect results?

Timing depends on the offer, market, buying cycle and available materials. We agree on an initial phase and progress indicators without promising sales numbers or closing dates.

How much does the service cost?

The proposal depends on markets, activities, languages and support required. We share scope and terms after the initial discussion and before work begins.
