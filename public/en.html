06 / Frequently asked questions

Let’s make things
clear.

Do you also deal with sales?+

Yes. We can cover the full cycle: customer research, outreach, qualification, sales calls, proposals, follow-up and closing support. At the start, we agree which stages we manage and which stay with your team.

Who do you work with?+

We work with B2B SMEs that have a proven product or service and want to develop new sales opportunities. The first conversation helps us understand your market, offer and goals, and assess where we can help.

How do you work with our team?+

We agree on roles, messaging and process. We share opportunity information and define who handles each stage, including technical assessments and final decisions on pricing and terms.

How much does it cost and how do you start?+

The cost depends on your market, the work required and the sales stages we cover. We start by discussing your business, then prepare a proposal with clear activities, responsibilities, timing and terms.

Can we start with the 120-day pilot?+

Our 120-day pilot is the starting programme for outbound acquisition. If you also want sales management, we agree on the scope and terms of the project together. You can explore the current outbound programme.

Do you also work on foreign markets?+

Yes. We also support international sales development. Together, we select your target markets and adapt research, messaging and the sales process to the local context.
