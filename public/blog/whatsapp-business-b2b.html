WhatsApp Business for B2B: a practical follow-up channel

Choose between the app and the Business Platform, connect conversations to your CRM and use WhatsApp where the contact has agreed to the channel.

At a glance. Use WhatsApp for an agreed conversation, with clear identity, ownership and a record in the CRM. A signal on another channel is not a substitute for permission.

Give WhatsApp a clear role

WhatsApp can make an existing business conversation easier: confirming an appointment, answering a product question or agreeing the next step. It also feels more personal than an email inbox, so relevance and permission deserve particular care.

A public phone number, a LinkedIn connection or an email open does not by itself establish permission to send promotional WhatsApp messages. Decide how permission is obtained and recorded before adding the channel to a workflow.

Business app or Business Platform?

RequirementBusiness appBusiness Platform
A small team managing conversations directlyA practical starting point with a business profile and response tools.May be more infrastructure than needed.
Shared workflow and CRM integrationCheck current device, integration and team capabilities.Designed for API-based workflows and connected systems.
Automation and templated messagesLimited to the app’s current features.Subject to platform messaging rules, templates and configuration.
CostCheck current app features and any related services.Budget for messaging, provider services, integration and support.

The choice should follow the operating requirement. Do not purchase an API integration just because it is available; first establish who owns conversations, what volume needs coordination and what the CRM should record.

Build permission into the handover

  1. Give the contact a clear choice to continue on WhatsApp.
  2. Record the permission and the context in the CRM.
  3. Explain who is writing and why, using the business identity.
  4. Keep the message focused on the agreed conversation.
  5. Make it easy to stop messages and respect the request across the workflow.

Connect the conversation to the account history

A useful integration links the WhatsApp contact to the correct company and CRM record. Incoming replies should update the owner, status and next action. A response on one channel should pause unrelated follow-ups elsewhere.

Define what happens when the number does not match an existing record, more than one colleague replies or a message fails. Keep an operator able to take over. A disconnected automation can make the customer repeat information the team already has.

Plan the real cost

Platform and provider pricing can depend on the message category, recipient market, usage and service package. Check the current official Meta pricing and the provider’s quote rather than using a fixed per-message figure from an old article.

Include integration, inbox management, template review, monitoring and the time spent answering. For a sales workflow, cost per useful conversation and meeting held is more informative than the lowest advertised cost per message.

Keep channel rules and commercial logic aligned

Check the current WhatsApp policies and applicable contact requirements for your intended use. A promotional campaign, a requested service response and an appointment reminder are different contexts. Do not assume that using the Business Platform makes a campaign appropriate or compliant.

  • Do not copy a long email into a personal messaging channel.
  • Do not trigger outreach solely because a tracking pixel registered an open.
  • Do not keep sending after a refusal or opt-out.
  • Do not leave the conversation visible only on one person’s phone.
  • Do not treat a read receipt as evidence of commercial interest.

Frequently asked questions

Can WhatsApp replace email and LinkedIn?

It serves a different role. Choose the channel with the contact and keep the account history coordinated across the team.

Does an email reply mean we can use WhatsApp?

Ask how the person wants to continue and record that choice. An interaction elsewhere is not an automatic permission for promotional messages on another channel.

When does CRM integration become useful?

When ownership, history and follow-up are hard to manage reliably in the app. Start with those operational needs and design the integration around them.
